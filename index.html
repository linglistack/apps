Privacy and support

This page belongs to the iPhone apps that link to it, from their App Store page and from the About screen inside each app. In short: what you put into these apps stays on your iPhone, and you can reach us by email.

SupportPrivacy policy

Support

A question, a problem, or something you wish the app did? Write to us.

hello@heyreagent.com

It helps to say which app it is, what you did, what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About).

How do I delete what I entered?

Remove it in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, when you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or transfer directly from the old one. The apps have no account and no syncing of their own.

Can I get a refund?

Payments on the App Store are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy policy

In effect from 12 October 2026.

What the apps collect

Nothing. More exactly:

  • There is no account and nothing to sign in to.
  • What you enter is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The apps do not send or receive anything over the internet. The link to this page opens in your browser, outside the app.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no code from other companies that collects data.

Permissions

Some of the apps ask to use the camera, the microphone, your location, the motion sensors, or to save a picture to Photos. An app asks only when a feature needs it and says why at that moment. What it reads is used on your iPhone for that feature and goes nowhere else. You can take a permission back at any time in Settings, under the app's name.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, what an app saved is part of that backup. The backup is kept by Apple or on your computer, under your own account, not by us.

What Apple tells us

Apple gives every developer totals such as how many times an app was downloaded, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements). These do not include your name or how to contact you. If an app has a price, Apple takes the payment; we never see your card.

Sharing with others

We receive no data from the apps, so there is none to share with, or sell to, anyone.

If you email us

We receive your email address and what you wrote. We use them to answer you and for nothing else. Ask us to delete the conversation and we will.

Keeping and deleting

What you enter stays on your iPhone until you remove it in the app or delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The apps are not made for children, and they collect nothing from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.

Who we are

The apps are published by the developer named as the seller on each app's App Store page. To reach us about privacy or anything else: hello@heyreagent.com.
